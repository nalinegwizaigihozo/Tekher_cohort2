COMMON QUESTIONS
----------------
Q: Why SVM?
A: "SVM is a supervised classifier that works well with high-dimensional
feature spaces and can learn non-linear boundaries using kernels."

Q: Why HOG?
A: "HOG converts visual information such as local edge and shape patterns
into numerical features that an SVM can process."

Q: What if the image is completely different from the training data?
A: "The prediction may be less reliable. The model learns patterns from its
training distribution, so real-world images can produce different results."

Q: What does the confusion matrix show?
A: "It compares the true class with the predicted class. Correct predictions
appear on the diagonal; off-diagonal values represent mistakes."

Q: What is 80/20?
A: "80 percent of the data is used for training and 20 percent for testing."
